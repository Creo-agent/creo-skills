# Ad Recommendation Guardrails for Localization

> **Owner:** Neta Ariely (Meta Agentic DACH)
> **Last updated:** 2026-07-09

These rules apply when selecting ads from other geos to recommend for DACH localization.

## Mandatory Filters

1. **No "llama" ads** — Exclude all llama-themed creatives from recommendations. Do not include any ad whose name or concept involves "llama".

2. **Launch date: after May 2026 only** — Only evaluate ads that were launched *after May 2026*. All recommendations must be based on recent, up-to-date creatives. Older ads are excluded regardless of performance.

## Rationale

Recommendations should reflect the current creative direction and recent learnings. Legacy creatives (pre-June 2026) and specific excluded themes (llama) are not candidates for localization.
